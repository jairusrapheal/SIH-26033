## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:
1. Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
2. Provides logistics support.
3. Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.
Benefits:
1. Better prices for farmers.
2. Lower prices for consumers.
3. Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmDirect is a digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, hotels, and bulk buyers. The main aim is to eliminate unnecessary middlemen and ensure that farmers receive better prices for their agricultural products while consumers get fresh products at affordable prices.
Farmers can register on the platform, upload details of their products, mention available quantities, set prices, and receive orders directly from buyers. Consumers can browse available products, compare prices, place orders, and make online payments.
The platform also provides logistics support by connecting farmers with delivery partners. An AI-based system helps predict market demand, suggest suitable prices, and identify efficient delivery routes.

## Technical Approach
The proposed platform will be developed as a web and mobile-friendly application using modern technologies.

Implementation Methodology:
The system will follow these steps:
1. Farmers register and create their profiles.
2. Farmers upload their available agricultural products.
3. Buyers search for products and compare prices.
4. Buyers place orders directly with farmers.
5. The system confirms orders and processes payments.
6. Logistics partners collect and deliver products.
7. AI analyzes previous sales and predicts future demand.
8. The system suggests optimized delivery routes to reduce transportation costs.

## Feasibility and Viability
armDirect is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available web technologies, smartphones, internet connectivity, and existing transportation services.
Technical Feasibility:
The application can be developed using Python, Django, React.js, and a relational database. These technologies are widely used and suitable for building scalable online marketplaces.
Economic Feasibility:
The initial prototype can be developed with low investment using open-source technologies. The platform can generate revenue through small transaction fees, logistics partnerships, and optional premium services for bulk buyers.
Operational Feasibility:
Farmers can use the platform through smartphones or assistance from local FPOs and agricultural organizations. Consumers can access the marketplace through a website or mobile application.
